Key Innovation

Clinical Implication

Every output is deterministic and auditable — not a neural network prediction.

Sensitivity Analysis: E-Value Robustness

VeriMath computed E-values to assess confounding resistance — how robust is the AVR benefit?

E-value = HR−1 + √(HR−1 · (HR−1 − 1))
FindingHRE-Value
(point)		E-Value
(CI)
AVR mort. (Oct)0.286.609.39
AVR mort. (Non-Oct)0.384.726.82

What E-Value = 6.60 Means

An unmeasured confounder would need to be associated with both AVR treatment and mortality by a risk ratio of at least 6.60 to explain away the observed HR of 0.28.


For Context

  • Smoking → lung cancer: RR ≈ 10–20
  • Obesity → diabetes: RR ≈ 3–7
  • Required confounder: RR ≥ 6.60 for both exposure and outcome

VeriMath Conclusion: It is implausible that unmeasured confounding alone explains the AVR mortality benefit. The finding is robust.
